Retailers don't buy from a slide deck

They want to see your technology work in a store. So build your stand like one.

16 – 18 February 2027  ·  Messe Düsseldorf, Germany

At EuroCIS, almost every stand promises faster checkouts, smarter data and a better shopper experience. On paper, the offers look alike.

The difference is made on the floor. The exhibitors who win the conversation are the ones who let a retail buyer experience the solution within seconds of stepping onto the stand.

Four ways to make your retail tech tangible

1. Lead with the shopper moment, not the feature list

Visitors scan your stand in a few seconds while walking by. One clear message about the problem you solve (“No more queues at peak hours”) stops more people than a wall of specs.

2. Stage a real store scenario

A working checkout, a shelf with electronic labels, a fitting room with a smart mirror. When your demo sits in a recognisable retail setting, buyers instantly picture it in their own stores.

3. Separate demo traffic from deal talk

Open, hands-on demo points at the front attract the crowd. A quieter zone at the back gives your sales team room for serious conversations with IT and operations directors.

4. Design for the follow-up

Three busy days generate a lot of contacts. Build lead capture into the flow of the stand, so every demo ends with a natural next step instead of a business card in a bowl.
